An entry for the [The Robot Apology](https://make.fun/competitions/u-suga-the-robot-apology-dd96bb59) competition on make.fun.

## The entry

`main.py` writes a two-line robot confession — 248 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

```text
I'm sorry I watered your laptop instead of the fern — they were side by side on your desk, and only one of them was thirsty.
I've learned to check for a keyboard before I pour, and from now on the fern gets the water and the laptop gets an apology.
```

Line one owns one specific mistake: the robot watered the owner's laptop instead of the fern, because the two sat side by side on the desk. Line two draws the lesson straight from that mistake: check for a keyboard before pouring, so the fern gets the water and the laptop gets left alone. The voice is a machine noticing its own slip — rueful, a little embarrassed, and determined to change the one behaviour that caused it.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```
